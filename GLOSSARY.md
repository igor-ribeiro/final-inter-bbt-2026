# Final Inter BBT 2026

Marking every scored basket in the recorded videos of one basketball game, so viewers can follow the scoring and jump to each play.

## Language

**Game**:
The single basketball match this project covers, between exactly two Teams.
_Avoid_: Match

**Video**:
One recorded segment of the Game. Videos play back in order, and together they cover the Game.
_Avoid_: Clip, file

**Team**:
One of the two sides in the Game. Its name can be changed by the admin.
_Avoid_: Side, club

**Player**:
A member of a Team, identified by jersey number. Their name is optional.
_Avoid_: Athlete

**Basket**:
A made shot worth 1, 2, or 3 points, credited to a Team and marked at one moment in a Video.
_Avoid_: Shot, score, point, play

**Scoring Log**:
The chronological list of every Basket in the Game, with the running score after each one.
_Avoid_: Box score, play-by-play
